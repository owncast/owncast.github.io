If you enjoy using Owncast, please consider giving it a star on GitHub. It only takes a second and helps support the project by sharing it with others. You can do so by visiting the [Owncast GitHub repository](https://github.com/owncast/owncast) and clicking the "Star" button.

While this is a bit of a vanity metric, it helps us get in front of people who might be interested in both using, and talking about Owncast. We don't get a lot of opportunities to reach new users, so every star helps us grow the community and spread the word.

Thank you for your support!
