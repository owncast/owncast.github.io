> Located at: [merch.owncast.online](http://merch.owncast.online)

Due to popular demand over the years, we're putting up a store to allow people to buy a T-shirt and maybe a couple other Owncast branded items. This will also be a way for people to support the project in a small way.

Releasing this in tandem with the Owncast 5-year anniversary in May 2025 would be a nice thing to do.

Each item will be marked up a few dollars, and this will be deposited directly into the Owncast Open Collective account via bank transfer.

## Owncat Mascot

While many use cases, audiences, and installations of Owncast are serious, others are fun. Many open source projects have some sort of fun mascot to use when appropriate.

Thanks to the generosity of [Mitch Effendi](https://posts.dumb.stuff.donaberger.xyz/@mitch), we have a fun cat mascot to use on branded items. This is "Owncat".

Owncat is a grinning cat, wearing a purple hoodie, emblazoned with the Owncast logo.

We haven't had any discussions on anything regarding this mascot, so everything is up in the air. How we refer to it (Owncat the streaming cat?), in what cases we use it, etc.
