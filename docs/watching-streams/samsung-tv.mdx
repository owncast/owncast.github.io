  <DeviceOptionCard
    id="browser-samsung"
    title="Built-in Internet Browser"
    difficulty="hard"
    summary="Try Samsung's preinstalled Internet app to open an Owncast stream directly on the TV."
  >
    <ul>
      <li>Samsung ships a preinstalled browser app called Internet.</li>
      <li>Browser capability and video support vary by model and firmware.</li>
      <li>No guarantees of compatibility.</li>
    </ul>

    <details>
      <summary>Show step-by-step instructions</summary>

      1. Press **Home**, move left in Smart Hub, select **Media**, then open the **Internet** app.
      2. Navigate to your Owncast server URL.
      3. Try to play the stream directly in the browser.

      **Note:** TV browsers are limited compared with a phone or computer, so web video playback may not work reliably.

    </details>

  </DeviceOptionCard>

  <DeviceOptionCard
    id="casting-samsung"
    title="Screen Mirroring/Casting"
    difficulty="medium"
    screenshot="/images/devices/samsung-tv/screen-mirroring.png"
    screenshotAlt="AirPlay device selection screen"
    summary="Use screen mirroring from your phone or computer to display Owncast streams."
  >
    <ul>
      <li>Works with Samsung's Smart View app or built-in screen mirroring.</li>
      <li>Requires a compatible smartphone or computer.</li>
      <li>May introduce some latency.</li>
    </ul>

    <details>
      <summary>Show step-by-step instructions</summary>

      1. Ensure your Samsung TV and device are on the same Wi-Fi network.
      2. On your phone or computer, open the Owncast stream.
      3. Use screen mirroring features:
         - **Android**: Use Smart View or Screen Mirroring
         - **iOS**: Use AirPlay (if supported by TV)
         - **Windows**: Use Project/Cast to Device
      4. Select your Samsung TV from the list of available devices.

    </details>

  </DeviceOptionCard>

  <DeviceOptionCard
    id="external-device-samsung"
    title="External Streaming Device"
    badge="Recommended"
    difficulty="easy"
    screenshot="/images/devices/roku/owncasts-roku-home.jpg"
    screenshotAlt="Owncasts for Roku home screen"
    summary="Connect a dedicated streaming device to your Samsung TV for better Owncast compatibility."
  >
    <ul>
      <li>Much more reliable than built-in Samsung TV apps.</li>
      <li>Wide variety of compatible apps and options.</li>
      <li>Better long-term support and updates.</li>
    </ul>

    Consider connecting one of these devices to your Samsung TV:
    - Apple TV
    - Roku
    - Amazon Fire TV Stick
    - Google Chromecast
    - NVIDIA Shield TV

    Then follow the instructions for your chosen streaming device.

  </DeviceOptionCard>
